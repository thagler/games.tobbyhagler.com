Orbit Queue

A spaceport full of little aliens.
A place for every passenger.

Tap a ship to board matching passengers. Plan around a crowded waiting area, make room for the next arrivals, and enjoy the small satisfaction of a ship ready to launch.

Settle into gentle music and colorful worlds, then change the pace with Fuel Lab puzzles and occasional visits from Admiral Grump.

How to play & support

In preparation for release. Requires iOS 17 or later.
Plays offline. No accounts, ads, or in-app purchases.

120 orbits

A full campaign of passenger-sorting puzzles, with six encounters with Admiral Grump along the way.

A change of pace

Visit the Fuel Lab every three orbits, and discover little facts between levels.

A gentle atmosphere

Five color palettes with accompanying soundtracks, simple sound controls, and gentle haptics you can switch off.
