Orbit Queue

Privacy policy

Effective September 29, 2026

Orbit Queue is designed to play offline without an account.

This policy covers the Orbit Queue app by Tobby Hagler / HaglerWare Ltd. and its pages at games.tobbyhagler.com.

Information in the app

The app stores game progress and settings on your device. It does not send those records to us. It has no advertising, tracking, third-party analytics, user accounts, or in-app purchases. We do not collect personal data through the app.

Locally stored information includes your campaign progress, rescued-passenger and gameplay state, soundtrack playback bookmarks, sound and haptic preferences, and whether the opening theme has played for the installed release. This information is used to resume and personalize your game.

Your choices and local data

You can change sound and haptic preferences in the app. Deleting the app removes its local data from that installation; backups may be retained or restored according to your Apple device and iCloud settings. Orbit Queue does not operate its own backup or cloud-sync service.

Apple may separately process App Store, purchase/download, device-backup, or diagnostic information under its own policies and your settings. See Apple’s privacy policy.

If you contact support

If you email us, we receive your email address, your message, and any attachments you choose to send. We use this information to respond and investigate the issue. Do not include passwords or other sensitive information. Contact us if you want to request deletion of support correspondence; we will handle the request subject to applicable legal and recordkeeping requirements.

This website

This website is hosted by Cloudflare. Delivering and protecting the site involves technical request information, such as IP addresses, requested pages, browser information, and request times. Cloudflare processes this information to provide its hosting and security services. See Cloudflare’s privacy policy.

We have not added advertising, analytics scripts, tracking pixels, contact forms, or a mailing-list signup to these pages. A small script reveals our contact email when you choose to show it; it does not send information or track visitors. External links, including email links and Apple pages, are handled by the services you choose to open.

Children’s privacy

The app does not ask players for personal information or require an account. If a child’s personal information has been sent to us in a support message, a parent or guardian can contact us to request its removal.

Changes and contact

We will update this page and its effective date if the app’s data practices change.
