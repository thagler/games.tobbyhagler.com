Getting started

How do I play?

Tap a ship to board passengers of the matching color. The waiting area holds passengers who cannot board yet. Keep space available, fill ships, and work through the arrivals. Use the question-mark button in the game for the full guide.

Can I play without internet?

Yes. Orbit Queue plays offline and does not require an account. Opening a support or privacy link takes you to this website and requires an internet connection.

How do I change the sound or haptics?

Open Help & Settings using the question-mark or gear button. The sound section lets you turn music and sound effects on or off and adjust their volumes. You can also turn gentle haptics on or off. Music includes the opening theme.

Why is the game silent?

Check the music and sound-effects switches and volume sliders in the game, your device volume, Silent Mode, and any connected headphones. Audio pauses when the app is in the background or another audio interruption occurs.

Where is my progress saved?

Progress and preferences are stored locally on your device. The game does not provide an account or its own cloud-sync service. Avoid deleting the app when troubleshooting, because deletion can remove your saved progress. Device backups are managed by Apple and your device settings.

Can I use iPad in either orientation?

Yes. Orbit Queue supports portrait and landscape on iPad. The interface also adapts to supported iPhone screen sizes.

What accessibility options are available?

The app includes VoiceOver labels, scalable interface text, and support for the system Reduce Motion setting. Some puzzles have timing or visual matching elements. If something prevents you from playing comfortably, please contact us with the screen and setting involved.

Before reporting a problem

Try closing and reopening the app, then check for an available update. If the problem continues, contact support before reinstalling so we can help protect your local progress.
